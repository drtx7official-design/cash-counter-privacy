Privacy Policy — Cash Counter

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Cash Counter respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Data Collection

Cash Counter does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account, login, name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.

Local Data Storage

Information entered into Cash Counter is stored locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties.

Internet Access

Cash Counter is designed to work offline and does not send user data over the internet.

Advertising

Cash Counter does not display advertisements and does not use advertising services to collect user information.

Data Deletion

Because app data is stored locally on the user's device, users can delete the locally stored app data through the Android device's app settings or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing for Cash Counter.
